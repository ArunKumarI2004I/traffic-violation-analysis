# TRAFFIC VIOLATIONS ANALYSIS
## Power BI Dashboard – Project Documentation

**Project Type:** Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

## 1. Introduction

The Traffic Violations Analysis project is a Business Intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. Its primary objective is to provide an analytical overview of traffic violations, associated fines, fine collection, and violation status.

The dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs) and graphical visualizations that enable users to understand violation patterns, monitor fine collection performance, and evaluate the distribution of violations across different statuses.

By consolidating these metrics into a single reporting interface, the project aims to make traffic violation information easier to interpret and support data-driven decision-making.

## 2. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Analyze the overall number of recorded traffic violations.
- Monitor the total monetary value of traffic fines.
- Evaluate the number of pending fines.
- Track fines that have been successfully paid.
- Identify violations that remain unaccounted for.
- Examine monthly trends in traffic violations.
- Understand the distribution of violations based on their current status.
- Present important analytical findings through an interactive dashboard.

## 4. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is organized into a single reporting page titled **Traffic Violations Analysis**.

Its subtitle identifies three major analytical areas:

- Monthly Trends
- Fine Collection
- Violation Status

The dashboard combines KPI cards, a clustered column chart, and a donut chart to provide a consolidated view of traffic violation statistics.

### 4.1 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard contains five KPI cards.

**1. Total Violations**

Displays the overall number of traffic violations recorded in the dataset.

Purpose:
- Understand the overall volume of traffic violations.
- Establish a high-level measure of violation activity.

**2. Total Fines**

Displays the aggregate monetary value of traffic fines.

Purpose:
- Monitor the financial value associated with recorded violations.
- Understand the overall fine amount.

**3. Pending Fines**

Displays the monetary value of fines that remain pending.

Purpose:
- Monitor outstanding fine amounts.
- Identify potential gaps in fine collection.

**4. Paid Fines**

Displays the monetary value of fines that have been paid.

Purpose:
- Track fine collection performance.
- Understand the amount successfully collected.

**5. Unaccounted Violations**

Displays the number of violations classified as unaccounted for.

Purpose:
- Monitor violations that have not been accounted for.
- Identify records requiring further attention.

## 5. Data Visualization and Analysis

### 5.1 Monthly Traffic Violations Analysis

**Visualization:** Clustered Column Chart

**Category Axis:** Month  
**Value Axis:** Total Violations

This visualization presents the number of traffic violations across different months.

It enables users to:
- Identify months with higher violation counts.
- Observe monthly variations.
- Compare traffic violation volumes across reporting periods.
- Identify potential seasonal or recurring patterns.

The monthly visualization provides a temporal perspective on traffic violation activity.

### 5.2 Violation Status Distribution

**Visualization:** Donut Chart

**Category:** Violation Status  
**Measure:** Violation Count by Status

This visualization represents the distribution of traffic violations across their respective status categories.

It enables users to:
- Understand how violations are distributed across different statuses.
- Compare the relative contribution of each status.
- Identify dominant violation statuses.
- Evaluate the overall status composition of recorded violations.

The donut chart provides a categorical overview of violation records.

## 6. KPI and Measure Reference

The following table summarizes the analytical measures referenced by the dashboard.

| Measure | Analytical Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Violations | Overall violation count |
| Total Fines | Aggregate fine amount |
| Pending Fines | Outstanding fine amount |
| Paid Fines | Collected fine amount |

## 8. Analytical Applications

The dashboard can support several analytical activities:

1. **Traffic Monitoring:** Understanding the overall volume of traffic violations.
2. **Financial Monitoring:** Evaluating the monetary value of fines and outstanding amounts.
3. **Collection Analysis:** Comparing paid and pending fine amounts.
4. **Temporal Analysis:** Identifying monthly variations in traffic violations.
5. **Status Monitoring:** Understanding the distribution of violations across status categories.

These applications help transform recorded traffic violation information into an accessible analytical report.

## 9. Expected Outcomes

The project provides a consolidated view of traffic violation statistics through visual reporting.

The expected outcomes include:

- Improved visibility into traffic violation volumes.
- Better understanding of monthly violation patterns.
- Easier monitoring of fine collection.
- Identification of pending financial obligations.
- Improved visibility into violation status distribution.
- A centralized reporting interface for analytical review.

## 10. Limitations and Future Enhancements

The current dashboard provides an overview of traffic violations, fine amounts, monthly trends, and violation status.

The following enhancements could extend its analytical capabilities:

- Introduce date-range and month filters.
- Add geographical analysis of traffic violations.
- Analyze violations by vehicle category.
- Analyze violations by violation type.
- Introduce fine collection percentage.
- Compare paid fines against pending fines.
- Add year-over-year and month-over-month comparisons.
- Introduce drill-through pages for detailed record-level analysis.
- Add additional KPIs for identifying collection efficiency and violation patterns.

These enhancements are recommendations and are not represented as existing dashboard functionality.

## 11. Conclusion

The Traffic Violations Analysis project demonstrates the application of Microsoft Power BI in transforming traffic violation records into meaningful visual insights.

By combining KPI cards, monthly trend analysis, and violation status distribution, the dashboard provides a centralized reporting environment for examining traffic violations and associated financial information.

The project establishes a foundation for further business intelligence development, enabling more detailed analysis of traffic patterns, fine collection performance, and violation management.
